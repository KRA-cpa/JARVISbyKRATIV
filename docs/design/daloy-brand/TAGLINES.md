# Daloy taglines

**Tone (user, 1 Nov 2026):** calm and productive, never "workaholic". Sell relief (no chasing, clear steps, time for
the real work), not relentless output. Avoid "never stops", "non-stop", "tuloy-tuloy", "hindi hinto", "go with the flow".

## In use
| Where | Line | Constant (`src/config/site.js`) |
|---|---|---|
| Home hero, login, page meta | **Less chasing, more flow.** | `BRAND_TAGLINE` |
| Footer, local posts | **Magaan ang daloy.** ("The flow is light / easy") | `BRAND_TAGLINE_LOCAL` |
| Under the name (home) | da-LOY · Tagalog for "flow" (pronunciation hint) | `BRAND_MEANING` |

## Alternates (approved tone)
**Ease / relief**
- Requests that find their way.
- Smooth from request to approval.
- Work, flowing easy.
- Swabe ang daloy. ("Smooth flow" - casual slang, keep out of formal sales copy)
- Sa Daloy, tuloy. ("With Daloy, it goes through" - *tuloy* = go ahead / come in)

**Productive work (focus, not hustle)**
- Focus on the work. Daloy handles the flow.
- Spend time on the work, not the follow-ups.
- Your work, in flow. (double meaning: "flow state" = focused, unhurried work)
- Clear steps. Fewer follow-ups. Work done.
- Mas tapos, mas magaan. ("More done, lighter load")
- Trabaho, hindi habol. ("Work, not chasing")

## Rules for claims
"Less chasing" holds because tickets route themselves and SLA escalations remind approvers. Don't attach numbers
("50% fewer follow-ups") without measured client data.
