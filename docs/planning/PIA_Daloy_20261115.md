# Privacy Impact Assessment — Daloy (DRAFT)

> Draft for the Data Protection Officer and counsel. `[[...]]` = to confirm. Repeat at each major version and yearly.

## §1 The system
Daloy is a multi-tenant ticketing and approval-workflow service. Customer organizations (employers) define request
types (purchase, leave, HR, payroll ...), approval steps and SLAs; their employees file tickets and approve them.
- **Roles:** the customer organization is the **personal information controller (PIC)** of its users' and tickets'
  data; KRAtiv is its **processor (PIP)** under the DPA contract. KRAtiv is the **PIC** of its own data: sign-in
  accounts, security logs, billing contacts, website enquiries.
- **Platform (after the migration):** Google Cloud Run + Cloud Scheduler (Singapore), Neon Postgres on AWS
  (Singapore), Firebase Hosting (website, no customer data stored), Google sign-in, KRAtiv's Google Workspace mailbox,
  Secret Manager, Cloud Logging [[Singapore once set]]. Before the move: Google Sheets / Apps Script. Customer-connected
  services (their own Drive, mail, Sheet) are under the customer's own agreements. **Vercel is not used** for the
  service; it hosts internal preview builds on test data only.

## §4 Risks
Likelihood (L) and impact (I): 1 low - 3 high.
| # | Risk | L | I | Controls | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | A user sees another person's ticket (wrong visibility) | 2 | 3 | server-side visibility on every read (compliance A21), Restricted tickets + masking, tests | low |
| R2 | Sensitive information outside Restricted ticket types | 2 | 3 | Restricted types / fields; admin guidance; customer instruction | medium - depends on the customer's setup [[customer onboarding note]] |
| R3 | Account takeover (stolen Google session) | 2 | 3 | Google sign-in, 12 h cap, 60 min idle sign-out, forced sign-out, invite allow-list | low |
| R4 | Platform admin misuse / compromise | 1 | 3 | no implicit access to tickets (support grants, PlatformManager §5), audit log, 2-step verification [[to confirm]], confidentiality undertaking | low once 2SV + undertakings are in place |
| R5 | Provider breach (Google, Neon) | 1 | 3 | encryption at rest / in transit, keys in Secret Manager, breach templates, forced sign-out | low |
| R6 | Real data in test environments | 2 | 2 | previews on test data (guide), Neon `dev` real rows only for the migration test, then replaced | medium until the previews are switched |
| R7 | Data kept too long | 2 | 2 | retention job, off-boarding tools, logs 30 days | low once the job is scheduled |
| R8 | Cross-border transfer | 1 | 2 | data in Singapore; sign-in on Google's global network; disclosed; DPA Annex B / C | low |
| R9 | Breach not reported in time | 1 | 3 | runbook, response team, templates, 72 h / [[24]] h clocks | low |

## §5 Security measures (summary)
Organizational: DPO, manual, undertakings, access review, training, breach team. Physical: providers' data centers
(Google, AWS). Technical: verified sign-in on the server, allow-list, role and platform checks on the server, session
limits and forced sign-out, Restricted data masking, audit logs, HTTPS, encryption at rest, secrets in Secret
Manager, least-privilege database roles [[confirm before 3.1]].

## §6 Actions
1. DPO appointed; this PIA signed. 2. Previews switched to test data; Neon `dev` real rows replaced after the 1.1 sync.
3. 2-step verification on platform-admin accounts. 4. Retention periods confirmed; retention job scheduled.
5. Cloud Logging bucket in Singapore. 6. Invitation emails with the privacy link (server invites).
7. Reassess at the first paying customer.

## §7 Conclusion
With the actions in §6, the residual risk is **low to medium**; the medium items (R2, R6) depend on customer setup
and on switching the previews. [[DPO to confirm]]
